# AI Feature Roadmap for CRM Lead Intelligence Platform

## Purpose

This document expands the current AI vision beyond the initial **Lead
Scoring** capability. It groups AI features into logical product areas
based on the historical CRM dataset (lead lifecycle, status history,
contact history, meetings, assignments, budgets, geography, tenant data)
and future WhatsApp engagement data.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. AI Sales Copilot (Sales Executives)

These features directly assist sales representatives while working
leads.

## 1.1 Lead Scoring (Current)

-   Hot / Warm / Cold categorization
-   Conversion probability
-   Confidence score

## 1.2 Explainable AI

Show *why* a lead received its score.

Example: - Budget matches successful buyers - Multiple successful
contacts - Site visit completed - Similar historical leads converted

## 1.3 Next Best Action

Recommend the most effective next step: - Call now - Schedule meeting -
Arrange site visit - Send brochure - Escalate to senior executive

## 1.4 Best Time to Contact

Predict the time of day or week with the highest likelihood of customer
response.

## 1.5 Follow-up Recommendation

Suggest: - Follow up immediately - Wait two days - Stop pursuing -
Re-engage after a specified period

## 1.6 Similar Converted Leads

Retrieve historical leads with similar characteristics that successfully
converted.

## 1.7 Lead Summary

Generate a concise AI summary of: - Customer profile - Journey - Current
stage - Risks - Recommended action

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. AI Sales Manager

Features focused on managers and team leaders.

## 2.1 Deal Risk Prediction

Predict the probability that a promising lead will be lost.

## 2.2 Drop-off Prediction

Identify leads likely to become inactive before they are marked as
dropped.

## 2.3 Salesperson Assignment Recommendation

Recommend the best salesperson based on historical performance with
similar leads.

## 2.4 Escalation Recommendation

Suggest when stalled leads should be reassigned or escalated.

## 2.5 Daily Priority Queue

Rank all active leads by urgency and expected business impact.

## 2.6 Pipeline Health

Detect unhealthy pipeline stages and identify where leads are being
lost.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. AI Analytics

Transform CRM history into business intelligence.

## 3.1 Lead Funnel Analytics

Measure: - New - Qualified - Meeting Scheduled - Site Visit -
Negotiation - Converted

## 3.2 Conversion Analytics

Per tenant: - Conversion rate - Stage conversion - Win rate

## 3.3 Marketing Source Intelligence

Evaluate marketing channels by: - Lead quality - Conversion rate - ROI

## 3.4 Geographic Intelligence

Compare: - Cities - Regions - Projects - Demand trends

## 3.5 Salesperson Performance Intelligence

Track: - Conversion rate - Response time - Productivity - Revenue
generated

## 3.6 Tenant Benchmarking

Compare tenants against platform averages.

## 3.7 Funnel Bottleneck Detection

Automatically identify stages where most leads are dropping.

## 3.8 Lead Journey Analytics

Analyze common customer journeys leading to successful conversions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Predictive Intelligence

## 4.1 Conversion Forecast

## 4.2 Revenue Forecast

## 4.3 Time-to-Conversion Prediction

Estimate how long each lead is expected to take before conversion.

## 4.4 Stage Duration Prediction

Predict expected time spent in each sales stage.

## 4.5 Customer Lifetime Value Prediction

Estimate long-term customer value.

# Vision Statement

CRM should evolve beyond a traditional CRM into an **AI-powered 
Intelligent Platform** where machine learning continuously
prioritizes opportunities, predicts outcomes, recommends actions,
automates repetitive work, and provides actionable insights for sales
executives, managers, and business leaders while maintaining strict
tenant isolation and cost-efficient AI serving.
